Method and limits

  • IKE parameters in this report are parsed from cleartext fields, not predicted. Each cites the packet it came from.
  • ESP and AH child SA parameters are negotiated inside encrypted exchanges. Where they appear at all, they were inferred from traffic metadata and carry a confidence; otherwise they are Unknown.
  • ESP key length cannot be recovered from a capture. AES-128 and AES-256 differ in round count, not in block or output size, so they produce identically shaped ESP payloads.
  • Anti-replay enforcement happens at the receiver and is not visible on the wire. Sequence numbers are reported as evidence only.
  • Confidences on inferred attributes are not yet calibrated against held-out data, so treat them as ordered rather than as probabilities.

Analysis warnings

  • The responder rejected 1 initial IKE_SA_INIT attempt(s) (INVALID_KE_PAYLOAD) before agreeing. A rejected key exchange group means the group finally used was the responder choice rather than the initiator preference, which is worth checking against policy.
